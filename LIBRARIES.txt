Note
- The installed OneWire 2.3.8 source emits two preprocessor warnings on this
  ESP32 core, but compilation and linking complete successfully.
- OneWire 2.3.8 bypasses the Nano ESP32 Arduino-pin remapping. This package
  therefore uses Dn/An aliases and requires the legacy GPIO numbering option.
